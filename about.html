About Back2U

Who We Are

Back2U is a university lost and found website that helps students reconnect with their missing belongings.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make finding lost items easier and help students return found belongings to their owners.

Why Back2U?

  • Easy to use for university students.
  • Saves time when searching for lost items.
  • Encourages students to help each other.
